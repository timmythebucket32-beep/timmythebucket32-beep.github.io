Projects

BDX Droid

A custom robotic droid designed and built using SolidWorks, 3D printing, ESP32-based electronics, motor control, and mechanical prototyping. The project focuses on integrating mechanical design, electronics, and motion control into a compact robotic platform.

High-Power Rocket

A high-power rocket designed around aerodynamic stability, structural design, propulsion, recovery-system sizing, and manufacturability using SolidWorks and OpenRocket.

Small-Scale Wind Tunnel

A small-scale wind tunnel designed in SolidWorks for aerodynamic flow visualization and testing of scaled vehicles, incorporating a honeycomb flow straightener, contraction section, test section, and diffuser.
